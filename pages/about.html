Family-run since 1995

Mimmo's has been part of St. Albans for three decades. We use only the freshest, highest-quality ingredients, prepared with care, to make dishes that keep our regulars coming back, whether they're craving New York-style pizza, a plate of pasta, a hero, a salad or a cannoli.

Our marinara, the way we've always made it

Our original marinara has been enjoyed in-house by our guests for over two decades. It's fresh, vine-ripened crushed tomatoes, onions, carrots, garlic, sea salt, spices, red wine and extra virgin olive oil. That's all there is to it: no preservatives, no sweeteners, no dairy, and no tomato paste.

Just like our food, the preparation, the attention to detail, and the hand of an experienced chef are what set it apart. Available only at our restaurant location.
